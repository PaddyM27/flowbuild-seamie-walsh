Our Story

A family business
built on beautiful homes

Seamie Walsh Interiors has been a fixture in Ardee since 1974. For over 50 years, families across Ireland have trusted us to help them create spaces they love — from a single statement sofa to a full interior transformation.

We carry a carefully curated selection of furniture and soft furnishings, and our in-house design service takes the stress out of putting it all together. We deliver nationwide.
